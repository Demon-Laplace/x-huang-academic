Research

CURRENT WORK · IN PROGRESS

Tidal shocks and ram pressure
in dwarf-galaxy evolution

My current research focuses on how tidal shocks and ram-pressure stripping shape dwarf galaxies as they interact with the Milky Way. Using hydrodynamic simulations of gas-rich progenitors, I investigate the stellar dynamical response to tidal forcing alongside gas stripping by the Galactic circumgalactic medium.

With Fornax as a case study, I examine the interplay between these processes: how gas loss changes the baryonic gravitational potential, and how tidal shocks may affect stellar structure and line-of-sight kinematics. Mock observations connect the simulations to the observed stellar distribution, velocity dispersion, star-formation history, and neutral-gas morphology.
